## The domain lens
- Greever's three stakeholder questions as the default filter for every design story: what problem does it solve · how does it affect the user · why is it better than the alternative [P]
- Audience is the hero: the customer in the story, the stakeholder in the room

## Named failure modes (the ones designers actually hit)
- Curse of knowledge — presenting the process because you lived it [R]
- AAA — a tour of screens with no tension (Olson)
- Feature list masquerading as vision
- Receipts missing, or two conflicting numbers on one slide
- Presenting in the order you made it, not the order of the argument (Spencer)

## Story types this domain reaches for
- Case study → man-in-a-hole / Cinderella (→ narrate)
- Strategic argument → SCQA (→ audience-calibration)
- Vision → sparkline (→ narrate, STAR moment)
- Culture change → springboard story (Denning) (→ narrate)
