# Find Story Opportunities
<!-- scaffold: T2, later. The find-animation-opportunities analogue — includes what NOT to tell. -->

## Inherits
- philosophy.md; the five questions as the filter

## Signals a story exists
- A turn (something changed) · a receipt (something measurable) · a person (someone specific) · a tension (something was at risk)
- Score each candidate on the four; fewer than three = not yet a story

## What to leave out (named)
- Vanity metrics with no turn · process detail with no decision · backstory nobody asked for · anything with two conflicting numbers

## Output format (required)
- Candidate list ranked; each: the turn · the receipt · the person · the tension · what's missing
- Explicit "not a story (yet)" list with the one thing that would make each one a story
